## In short

My City is a game for children. The app does not collect personal information, does not send any data off the device and does not need an account. Everything the app saves stays on the device.

## What is stored, and where

| What | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Game progress: the city, unlocked games, high scores and achievements | To continue where the child stopped | The app's local storage on the device (localStorage) |
| Settings: sound, vibration, graphics quality, the look of the character, a name chosen from a fixed list | To adapt the game | The same local storage |

This data never leaves the device. We run no server and have no access to it. It is not part of the device cloud backup (Android automatic backup is turned off in the app).

## What the app does not do

- No account, sign-up or login.
- No collection of name, age, address, location, phone number, photos or recordings.
- No access to the camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos.
- No ads, no advertising ID, and no third-party analytics or tracking libraries.
- No links out of the game and no in-app purchases.
- No network requests. All game files are inside the installed package.

## Read-aloud

Some text is read aloud on the device by the operating system's speech engine, and only with a Hebrew voice that is installed on the device. The text is not sent to any server.

## Clipboard

On the parents screen a parent can copy the progress data to the device clipboard. This happens only when the user taps the button, and the data stays in the device clipboard.

## Children

The app is intended for children (target age: ___ , fill in once decided). Because no personal information is collected, no parental consent for collection is needed. The app has no features that let a child communicate with other people.

## Deleting data

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data. Data can also be cleared in the device's app settings.

## Changes

If the app changes so that it collects any information, we will update this policy first and show the date at the top of the page.
