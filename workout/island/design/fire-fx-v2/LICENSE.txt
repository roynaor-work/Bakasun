Fire FX v2 — 2026-11-17

The fire, smoke, steam, ember, spark, soot, heat-mask and ignition artwork
was created for this package with OpenAI image generation. No stock art,
third-party textures, game artwork, fonts or branded material was imported.
The generated source was technically packed and WebP encoded with sharp.
The preview building consists of original simple Canvas geometry.
