Provenance
----------
All effect artwork is newly authored deterministic procedural artwork defined
in tools/build_assets.py. No downloaded photographs, third-party sprite packs,
AI image outputs, existing repository images, fonts, car models or logos were
used as image inputs. The preview draws only a simple material test surface
and an exhaust attachment fixture, not a car or a game scene. It uses system
fonts and native browser APIs. Python/numpy/Pillow and Playwright are optional
authoring/verification dependencies and are not bundled or needed at runtime.
