# Descriptive far-boot clearance

This RAM-pinned probe measured 96 foot samples across the 12 pitch actors and all six field identities, at cold idle, steady idle, steady run 3 and settled stop. It evaluates the actual displayed rigid Cloth/Foot vertices, rather than treating the canonical sole marker as the shoe surface.

The far boot's lowest rest vertex is about 5.128mm above the rig's zero plane. In the sampled planted poses, its displayed minimum remains the same within numerical precision; the marker and anchor are at zero, and the model has zero runtime floor lift. With turf at Y 1mm, the resulting surface gap is about 4.128mm. This explains the pitch audit's positive mesh minimum as simplified boot geometry rather than an accumulated body-height correction.

This is descriptive geometry evidence, not a new visible-hover failure, performance measurement or gate exemption. No conclusion about perceptibility at gameplay distance is inferred from a millimetre value alone. The proof covers the sampled far models and poses; it does not replace the final all 16 floor and transition checks. No source, motion, material or model file changed, and the browser closed with zero page errors or remote requests. Exact model/runtime response hashes and end-of-run stability are in `surface-gap.json`.
