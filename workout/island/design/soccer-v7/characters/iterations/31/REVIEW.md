## Selection after causal review

`preflight/macro-projected31/` contains2 actual P04 portrait controls, locked to the macro camera. Changing crown vertex normals from80% projected/20% own to100% projected leaves the tall ruled tines visible. This rejects a vertex-normal explanation for the remaining geometry/alpha silhouette. The selected consolidation therefore keeps standard20% own-normal blending, applies the8mm macro sweep only to P03, and preserves P04's shorter crown29+wisps31 without the14mm macro deformation. No global8/10 hair score is supported by these images. Cloth finishing remains a separately reviewed correctness candidate.
