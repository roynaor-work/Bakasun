# Independent static cloth review

Reviewed all 16 paired P01/P04 PNGs under `preflight/cloth-static-baseline/` and `preflight/cloth-static-finish/`: front and three-quarter views at 390×844 and 844×390. This is a static candidate comparison; the kick/get-up action captures remain separate evidence.

The finish gives the collar a smoother, more continuous binding. The isolated jagged neck corners are reduced, without a new obvious gap in the inspected poses. The upper-body folds remain restrained. Both kits retain a readable fine weave and subtle seams.

The independent cloth estimate remains about **7.5/10**. Broad chest and abdomen drape still looks smooth and rigid, with limited tension and fold structure at the shoulder, underarm and waist. Tiny dark rough flecks remain along sleeve and hem edges in close views. These improvements do not establish an 8/10 material-and-deformation result.

The action proof must separately show collar, shoulder, crotch and hem behavior during kick and get-up. Collision statistics alone cannot show natural cloth drape. This review makes no animation, whole-roster, lighting-mode or mobile-performance claim.

## Paused action comparison

Also reviewed all 16 P01/P04 paired kick-strike and supported-get-up PNGs under `preflight/cloth-action-baseline/` and `preflight/cloth-action-finish/`, at both phone sizes. No obvious new skin-through-cloth gap is visible in these selected poses. The collar finish is a small local change; the get-up still has angular shoulder/back peaks and broad polygonal folds. Those visible deformation issues retain the approximately 7.5/10 estimate.

The kick frames show a distant whole character and cannot resolve fine cloth detail. The portrait get-up frames clip part of the head and right hand, while landscape get-up deliberately crops the body to the torso. They establish selected cloth regions, rather than complete support or floor clearance. Decoded geometry checks and continuous motion remain separate obligations.
