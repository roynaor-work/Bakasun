# Upper-sock fit 21: rejected first preflight

The first attempted fit transfers actual anatomical skin weights to the cuff. The supported get-up phone frame exposes a failure: long radial rays cast against the whole body can intersect the opposite calf before the intended calf. The resulting inner sock points and weights bridge the knees. This is visibly worse than the original flared cuff and is rejected; the candidate is not promoted.

The correction isolates the intended anatomical leg before building the raycast BVH and explicitly rejects opposite-leg skin weights. A subsequent combined candidate must be rendered in the same deep crouch before acceptance. Boot, stud, sole geometry and the canonical skeleton are unchanged. Numeric bind assertions alone do not establish the corrected silhouette.

`actions/` preserves 12 original phone frames at both requested sizes, including the failed get-up. This is a partial diagnostic rather than a full accepted iteration. The live 15 assets remained frozen.
