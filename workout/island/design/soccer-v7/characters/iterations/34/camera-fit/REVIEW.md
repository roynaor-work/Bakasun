# Whole-head camera fit

The earlier face minimum protected vertical height but could crop the nose and rear skull at a 90° portrait profile. The corrected minimum fits both the padded head height and the projected horizontal head width, then adds the complete projected view-depth half extent. Physics, body/cloth cameras and motion timing are unchanged.

The actual native32 P01/P03/P04 were captured at front, three-quarter and 90° in 390×844 and 844×390. All 18 complete projected head boxes fit both axes. The closest portrait profile side margin is 23.89 px; the smallest landscape crown/chin margin is 32.36 px. Camera aspect was synchronized with the actual canvas before measuring or capturing. I inspected P01's full profile and P03's front framing: the nose, rear skull, crown and chin are visible, with a readable face. The portrait profile necessarily leaves more vertical room to preserve its full horizontal silhouette. A deliberate tighter user zoom can still crop the head.

`camera-fit.json` binds the runtime/model/package hashes and every PNG hash. The earlier unsynchronized metrics are archived separately and are not acceptance evidence. Final34 package photos and movies must be newly rendered; old33 frames are not relabeled.
