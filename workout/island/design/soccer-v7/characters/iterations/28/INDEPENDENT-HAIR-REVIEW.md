# Independent Hair28 review: P03 and P04

Inspected all 16 paired PBR phone images in `preflight/baseline26/` and `preflight/groom28/`: P03/P04, front/three-quarter, 390×844/844×390. Face25/26 geometry, maps, cap and material are shared by the two versions. This was a read-only image review, separate from the running numerical controller audit.

| Character | Visible gain | Tradeoff and remaining gap | Independent hair estimate |
|---|---|---|---|
| P03 | Slightly less regular fringe placement and a small change to the surface grouping. | The front portrait has some larger, more conspicuous pointed card tips. The broad smooth cap and cutout edge remain evident; the landscape views are close to the baseline. | Approximately 7/10; no demonstrated rise to 8. |
| P04 | The crown's straight bright streaks and repeated rows are a little less even; the three-quarter silhouette has slightly more variation. | Several long straight/diagonal card streaks remain, and parts of the fringe become broader pointed teeth. The material still reads as a smooth cap with laid-on groups at the requested full-phone portrait size. | Approximately 7/10; modest local improvement, still below 8. |

Hair28 is a small groom variation with a mixed fringe tradeoff, not a clear close-up quality breakthrough. P04's crown improvement is useful; P03's front edge needs finer tip groups before applying the change indiscriminately across the roster. The pictures do not justify an 8/10 hair claim or a face-score increase. They also do not establish the appearance of an unseen Face27+Hair28 combination.

The next correction should reduce the most conspicuous straight card streaks and preserve finer, less triangular fringe tips while keeping the improved variation. Moving deformation, shadows, floor clearance and mobile GPU performance require their separate checks.
