# Waist36 bounded regression evidence

The adopted change blends upper-shorts skin weights toward the jersey Hips/Spine rule over restY 0.70–0.84 m. Geometry, UVs, triangle indices, maps and canonical bones remain unchanged. This packet measures the actual exported P01 near/far candidate against the retained baseline using 68 frozen controller poses (17 motor-film frames and 17 clips at three phases), for 272 model/pose evaluations. It is a local regression packet, not a canonical all-character acceptance report.

Primary cloth evidence is [the FrontSide regression](cloth-raster-frontside-regression.json) and [pixel-location deltas](cloth-raster-frontside-local-delta.json). Their 136 baseline/candidate comparisons reduce projected shorts-before-shirt pixels from **58,703 to 22,609**. No pose/LOD changes from zero to positive, and no total count increases. Movie frames 91 and 165 remain clear in the candidate at both LODs. There are still 49 positive candidate masks: projection can include a folded leg legitimately in front of a shirt, so these counts do not establish physical penetration or universal clearance.

Local pixel differences retain 170 newly occupied pixels versus 36,264 cleared pixels. Five pose/LOD cases have new connected groups of at least four pixels: get_up at 0.2 (both LODs), get_up at 0.5 (far), and slide_tackle at 0.8 (both). The largest is 43 pixels. These are explicitly retained for paired action-image review, not hidden by the lower aggregate. The real [frame91 pair capture](../capture.json) and [retained get-up action image](../actions-unlocked-camera/candidate-get_up-0.2-lod1-390x844.png) are outside this numerical packet.

The delivered-Skin evidence is [the local ray report](skin-local-regression-result.json) and [its summary](skin-summary.json). Both baseline and candidate have zero locally corresponding Skin-before-shorts ray witnesses. The heuristic requires source-rest separation ≤40 mm and camera-depth gap ≤40 mm, excludes hands/opposite-leg crossings, and restricts delivered upper-leg Skin to restY 0.615–0.667 m. Raw silhouette overlaps rise 3,594→4,857, but sampled surfaces are far apart; they are retained in [raw results](skin-regression-result.json) and [separation examples](skin-ray-separation-examples.json). No hidden reference body is used. Zero local witnesses is not a full three-dimensional collision guarantee.

Two negative diagnostics remain visible. [The old fixed-pose floor check](floor-fixed-pose-FAILED.json) found **two regressions**, with a minimum candidate Cloth Y of **−14.54 mm** under frozen baseline skeletal matrices. It is retained as failed, does not apply the adopted candidate runtime floor correction, and is superseded for acceptance by the fresh canonical floor envelope and separate actual-runtime C16 proof. At archiving, the canonical envelope is PASS/complete and binds the adopted P01 near model; this old fixed-pose failure is still retained verbatim as a negative diagnostic. [The initial both-winding cloth raster](cloth-raster-regression.json) and [its delta](cloth-raster-local-delta.json) are superseded: actual Cloth is FrontSide. Their apparent dive-right increases came from culled backface projections. Primary FrontSide measurements show no total increase.

This packet covers one identity, fixed cameras and discrete poses. Far decoded geometry uses the same captured camera/bones as near; it is not a separately recorded far film. It does not establish all-eight-identity motion clearance, continuous transition quality, an art score of eight, physical-phone 30fps, or final package acceptance. Those belong to the final live audits and image review after adoption.

The archive index binds every retained file by current relative path and SHA256. JSON receipt paths resolve locally; historical producer paths/hashes are labeled notes. Producer scripts are byte-exact `.py.txt` records, including their historical execution paths; they are not runtime code. The frozen numerical input files retain their original bytes. No source, model, manifest or runtime file was changed while archiving.
