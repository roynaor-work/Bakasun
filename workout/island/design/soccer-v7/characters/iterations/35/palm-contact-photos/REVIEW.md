# Physical palm closeups

Eight side closeups use the final EF/f4d package, P02 LOD0, and both requested phone sizes: native slide, inherited slide recovery, and the two native get-up hands. `side/capture.json` records each actual active base, source clock, model hash, real triangle support, diagnostic camera and measured mesh minimum. There were no page errors or remote requests. The diagnostic camera changes framing only; poses come from the frozen controller.

At this magnification the hands remain visibly angular and the finger pose is rigid. The canonical18-bone skeleton has no finger articulation; these images do not establish an EA FC Mobile level hand-detail result. Surface support is established by the named skinned triangle and independent geometry checks, rather than by the soft shadow alone. Full-body naturalness and transitions are assessed separately in the eight motion films.

The initial angle hid the heel behind fingers and was discarded from acceptance review. Its first PNG and screenshot-timeout note remain as historical framing evidence. The successful side set uses a longer screenshot timeout and renders only the inspected final poses; it is not an FPS measurement.
