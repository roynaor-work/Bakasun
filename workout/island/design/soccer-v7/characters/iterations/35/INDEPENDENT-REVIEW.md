# Final35 · independent final-photo review

**The requested 8/10 artistic threshold is not reached.** Recommended final-photo grades are faces 7.5, hair 7.0, cloth 7.5 and combined lighting/shadows 7.5. These are visual judgements of the delivered native32 art in the final packaged viewer; they are not rounded up because codec, rig, contact or performance checks pass. No new art variant is proposed here.

## Scope and method

The reviewed set is exactly the 130 actual lossless WebP originals listed by [roster/capture.json](roster/capture.json): eight identities × front face, three-quarter face (`profile`), 90° face (`side`), stadium face, near LOD0 body, far LOD1 body, front cloth and three-quarter cloth, each at 390×844 and 844×390, plus two pitch images. All 130 files were checked for their recorded pixel dimensions. The final runtime/data and all 16 model hashes still match the capture when this review was written; errors and remote requests are empty.

The parent asset reviewer inspected all 64 face/stadium originals through labeled near-native contact sheets, then selected original phone files at native size. The atlas reviewer independently inspected all 64 cloth/body/far originals in both orientations and selected eight original files at native size. Both pitch originals were inspected. Contact sheets were temporary layouts of the actual originals, without recoloring, retouching or substituted renders; they are not new production frames. The selected original files and their SHA256 are listed below. Asset/atlas contributors participated in earlier authoring, so this is a second-agent final-art review rather than an external blind panel.

The rubric is [REFERENCE.md](../../REFERENCE.md). Its stated limits still apply: the official reference pages informed the quality target, but this is not a same-camera capture of EA FC Mobile on a phone. Static idle photos do not establish moving garment deformation, temporal hair shimmer, continuity, contact during actions or physical-phone 30fps. Those require the final movies and physical audits; no transition/floor score is inferred here.

The old large far-cap holes are absent for all eight identities in both orientations. Collar improvement is real, but a positive clearance guard is not a proof of sewn endpoint closure; small notches/dents remain visible. Neither rejected art study is included as a final improvement.
