All sound waveforms are generated from mathematical oscillators and seeded
pseudorandom noise. No recordings, external samples, speech models, music,
branded signals, team chants or recognizable announcer voices are used.
Crowd vowels are synthetic formant voices, not recordings of people.

Python, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib and FFmpeg are build tools. Their own licenses
remain applicable to those tools; they are not bundled in this directory.
