Changes (by the racing v7 package, design/racing-v7): kept the external body surfaces only; removed interior, engine, mechanics, wipers, plate and hidden parts; removed all source textures, material variants and logos; made five proportion / cabin variants and an open roadster; neutral materials, original lamps, rims, tyres and aero parts; separate wheel pivots; three geometry LODs with Draco compression. Full details: design/racing-v7/LICENSES.md and design/racing-v7/source/car-provenance.json.

The source distribution excludes logos and trademarks from its CC licence; none are included here. No endorsement by the original creator or DGG is implied.

The spectator figure (`crowd/`), prop library (`props/`), asphalt maps and tire-smoke sprite (`textures/`) were created for the package and are not photographs, scans of real people or branded assets.
