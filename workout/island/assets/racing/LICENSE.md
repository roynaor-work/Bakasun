Changes for Island Racing: removed interior/mechanical meshes and wipers; removed all material variants; replaced logo-bearing license plate and tire materials with neutral unbranded PBR materials; changed glass to opaque smoked glass; unified wheel metal materials; preserved exterior body proportions and paint/clearcoat; straightened and centered four independent wheel pivots; baked orientation to Y-up, +Z forward; joined, welded, reduced geometry, and resized retained textures to WebP at up to512×512. Detailed hashes and geometry metrics are in sport-coupe.provenance.json.
